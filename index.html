ARoL Service Description

at its basic level …

A caller – attempting to reach a roaming callee (ARoL subscriber) – is informed about the roaming country and the local time there. No matter if the caller and the callee are subscribers of the same Mobile Operator or not.

at its advanced level …

Additionally, the caller is offered the option of assuming roaming charges, allowing the callee to receive the call free of charge – provided that both (the caller and the callee) are subscribers of the same Mobile Operator.
